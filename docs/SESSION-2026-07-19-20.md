## 2026-07-20 — Schipal-Chronik (v1.2) & the learnbot cross-connect

- Edna will want to TALK about Onkel Franz. Fleet "collectively knows" ≠ architecture:
  build ONE corpus — `schipal-chronik` repo, markdown person/event/place pages,
  provenance frontmatter (who/when/certainty), photos by Immich asset ID.
- Consumers: Ednaficator RAG (direct), learnbot-mcp (later), thin chronik-MCP for
  Claude Desktop. Three doors, one corpus.
- **Capture loop:** gaps become prompts ("erzähl du mir vom Franz!") → voice note →
  transcript → Sandra-gated intake. Edna is consumer AND prime source.
- **HARD RULE:** corpus-only answers; improvised family history is poison.
- Start: 5 hand-written person pages before any ingestion code.

## 2026-07-20 — The 50-year principle

- Sandra: "in ten years I myself might be Edna, asking about my dog Lara from 1975."
- Consequence in PRD: the corpus must outlive the bot/fleet/Goliath. Plain markdown +
  JPEG + SQLite, readable 2076 with no service. Repo = heirloom; bot = current reader.
  Chronik captures Sandra's generation NOW, firsthand — Lara page written in 2026,
  not reconstructed in 2036.
